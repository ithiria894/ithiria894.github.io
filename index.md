## Anthropic IPO: day-one forecast, made before pricing

On 2026-11-16, two days before Anthropic set its IPO price and three days before ANT started trading on Nasdaq, I published and registered this forecast: **ANT closes its first trading day +15% to +25% above the IPO price.** I built it from 60 IPOs that raised $5B or more since 1996.

| | |
|---|---|
| Final IPO price (SEC 424B4, filed 2026-11-19) | $83.00 |
| Nasdaq close, 2026-11-19 | $97.17 |
| First-day return | **+17.07%** |
| Forecast +15% to +25% | **Hit** |

The report also said "Don't chase it on day one." The first trade was $111.50, 34% above the IPO price. Anyone who bought that first trade was down 12.9% at the close.

A second forecast is still open: the highest price of the first 60 trading days comes on or before trading day 10. It gets scored after trading day 60.
